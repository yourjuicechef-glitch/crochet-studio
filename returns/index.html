Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

Returns

Items may be returned within 3 days of delivery. To request a return, please contact us with your order details before sending an item back.

Return postage is borne by the customer. Where applicable, we may adjust return-postage costs from the refund settlement.

Custom and bulk orders

Custom and bulk orders are not eligible for returns or refunds.

Refunds

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received by us and inspected.

Cancellations

An order may be cancelled only before it has been shipped.

Need help?

For assistance, visit Contact Us. See Shipping & Delivery and Terms & Conditions.

How a refund reaches you

  • Cancelled before dispatch: the full amount is refunded.
  • Returned within 3 days of delivery: refunded once the piece has reached us and been inspected, less return postage where that applies.
  • Every refund goes back to the payment method you paid with, through Razorpay. Once it is issued, your bank usually takes 5–7 working days to show it.

See also the terms and conditions.

Not sure which one?

Tell us who it is for and roughly what you would like to spend, and we will suggest pieces and colours — with the price, before you decide anything.
