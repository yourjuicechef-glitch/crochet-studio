Crochet, crafted differently.

Crochet Studio creates handcrafted crochet clothing, accessories, toys and gifts for people who value distinctive design and thoughtful workmanship. Every piece is developed with attention to texture, colour, comfort and finishing.

It starts with yarn, a hook and a pair of hands

The journey of an order doesn’t begin with a machine or a product picked off a warehouse shelf. Once an order comes in, the first step is understanding exactly what needs to be made — the product, colour, size, design and any special details. Then comes the yarn. Choosing the right yarn is more important than it might seem: the colour needs to be right, but so do the texture, thickness and overall feel.

Then the real work starts

Crochet is created one stitch at a time. This part can take hours or days. Once the main crocheting is finished, the piece still isn’t ready to leave — the shape and details are checked. Handmade products naturally have character and small variations, but we still want every piece to be something we are proud to send.

An address is no longer just an address

Then comes one of our favourite parts: getting it ready for you. The piece is carefully packed and prepared for its journey. For the customer this might look like placing an order and waiting for a delivery. For us there is an entire story in between. That is the beauty of buying handmade.

Custom and bulk orders

Looking for a particular colour, size or design? We accept custom orders, and we work with companies and event organisers on personalised bulk gifting for employees, clients and special occasions. We currently serve customers across India.

Not sure whether something is possible? Message us — we will tell you honestly whether we can make it, what it will cost and how long it will take.
