Why handmade

Choose handmade crochet crafted in India — distinctive pieces made stitch by stitch for thoughtful gifts and everyday joy.

A scrunchie is an evening. A gajra is a day. There is no machine that does this — only someone sitting down with a hook and finishing what they started. Ready pieces dispatch in 2–4 working days. Made-to-order pieces are confirmed by WhatsApp before work starts.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing is made until you say yes.

  1. 1

    Pick a piece, or describe one

    Buy a ready piece straight from the shop — or send us a photo, a colour or an idea for something made to order.

  2. 2

  3. 3
