<p>At that age, I don&#8217;t think I fully understood how special those moments were. I was simply fascinated by the idea that with a hook, some yarn, and a little patience, I could create something with my own hands.</p>
<p>For a few years, crochet stayed quietly in the background. Then, when I was 20 and in college, I found myself drawn back to it. This time, however, something felt different. <p>And somewhere along the way, that little girl learning from her grandmother grew into someone who wanted to share those creations with others. That is a huge part of what Crochet Studio means to me.</p>
