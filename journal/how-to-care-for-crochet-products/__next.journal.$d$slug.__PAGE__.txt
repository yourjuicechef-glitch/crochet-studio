<p>Crochet products are special because they are made one stitch at a time. Whether you own a handmade crochet top, a cute crochet bag, a colourful scrunchie, a flower bouquet or a decorative coaster, every piece deserves a little care to keep it looking its best.</p>
<p>Unlike many factory-made products, handmade crochet items can have delicate stitches, different yarn textures and unique construction details. The way you wash, dry and store them can make a big difference to how they look and how long they last. The good news? Caring for crochet products does not have to be complicated. With a few simple habits, you can keep your favourite handmade pieces clean, maintain their shape and enjoy them for a long time.</p>

<h2>Why crochet care matters</h2>
<p>Proper crochet care helps you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maintain the original shape and fit of crochet clothing.</li>
<li>Keep crochet bags, pouches and accessories looking neat.</li>
<li>Prevent unnecessary wear and damage to delicate stitches.</li>
<li>Preserve the appearance of crochet flowers and decorative pieces.</li>
<li>Keep handmade products clean and ready to use.</li>
<li>Get more enjoyment from the pieces you have purchased.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Know your yarn</h2>
<p>Different yarns respond differently to water, heat and movement.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cotton yarn</strong> often handles gentle washing well, but the finished item can still stretch or lose its shape if handled roughly.</li>
<li><strong>Acrylic yarn</strong> may be suitable for gentle washing, depending on the product. High heat can damage or distort the fibres.</li>
<li><strong>Wool yarn</strong> can shrink or felt when exposed to unsuitable temperatures and agitation, so it often requires particularly gentle handling.</li>
<li><strong>Mixed-fibre yarn</strong> needs care based on the fibre blend and the manufacturer’s instructions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Caring for each kind of piece</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Crochet tops and clothing:</strong> wash according to the yarn instructions and dry flat to help maintain the fit.</li>
<li><strong>Crochet bags and pouches:</strong> spot-clean when suitable, avoid overloading them and store them neatly.</li>
<li><strong>Crochet flowers and bouquets:</strong> dust gently and protect the petals and stems from crushing.</li>
<li><strong>Crochet scrunchies:</strong> avoid rough pulling and follow the yarn-specific washing instructions.</li>
<li><strong>Crochet headbands:</strong> handle gently and store flat or in a way that protects their shape.</li>
<li><strong>Crochet coasters:</strong> clean spills promptly and allow them to dry fully before stacking.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Caring for a Crochet Studio piece</h2>
<p>Our pieces are made in cotton yarn. Hand wash them gently in cool water with a mild detergent, never wring, twist or bleach them, press the water out between two towels, reshape while damp and dry flat in the shade. Fold clothing rather than hanging it, because a hanger stretches crochet. The <a href="/crochet-studio/care">care guide</a> has the full instructions, and any instructions that come with your piece take precedence.</p>
