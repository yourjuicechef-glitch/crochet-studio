The Vision Behind Crochet Studio

The Crochet Studio workroom

Written from the table where the pieces are made · 1 September 2026

Crochet Studio began with a love for crochet, but my vision for it extends far beyond simply selling handmade products.

My vision is to create crochet pieces that feel personal. Products that bring warmth into a home, become meaningful gifts, make someone smile, or simply add a little beauty and character to everyday life.

I don’t want Crochet Studio to become a place where products exist simply because they can be sold. I want every collection to feel considered.

Crochet Studio has its roots in something deeply personal: learning crochet from my grandmother as a child, rediscovering it at 20, and eventually realising how much this craft had become a part of my identity.

Years from now, I hope someone can pick up a Crochet Studio piece and immediately feel that it wasn’t created to be ordinary or disposable. I hope they can see the care in the stitches, feel the quality of the work, and know that real time went into making it.
