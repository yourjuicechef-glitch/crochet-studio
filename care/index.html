Crochet Care Guide

Thoughtful care helps handmade crochet clothing, toys and accessories retain their shape and finish.

  • Check the product-specific care instructions first.
  • Hand wash gently in cool water with a mild detergent unless stated otherwise.
  • Do not wring, twist or bleach.
  • Reshape while damp and dry flat away from direct sunlight.
  • Store clothing folded rather than on a hanger to reduce stretching.
  • Keep toys and decorative pieces away from sharp objects and open flames.

Contact Crochet Studio if you are unsure how to care for a particular yarn or product.

At a glance

  • Hand wash

    Wash gently by hand with a mild detergent — never in a machine.

  • Cool water

    Cool water only. Warm water relaxes cotton stitches out of shape.

  • Do not wring

    No wringing, twisting or bleaching. Press the water out between two towels.

  • Dry flat

    Reshape while damp and dry flat, so the piece keeps the shape it was made in.

  • Out of the sun

    Dry in the shade. Direct sunlight fades dyed cotton.

  • Store folded

    Fold clothing rather than hanging it — a hanger stretches crochet.

  • Away from flame

    Keep toys and decorative pieces away from sharp objects and open flames.
