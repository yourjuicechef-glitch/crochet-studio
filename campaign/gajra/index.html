What it is

Inspired by the beauty of traditional flower gajras, our crochet version is made to be worn and loved again and again. Carefully crocheted flowers come together to create a delicate accessory that adds a beautiful handmade touch to buns, braids, and festive hairstyles. Unlike fresh flowers, these little blooms stay with you long after the occasion is over.

Looking after it

  • Hand wash

  • Cool water

  • Do not wring

  • Dry flat

  • Out of the sun

  • Store folded

  • Away from flame

Questions

How long is the gajra?+

The standard length suits a bun or a plait. Tell us how long you need it and we will work it to that — it is made to order either way.

Can I order several for a wedding?+

Yes. For more than a handful, the bulk gifting page gets you a quotation with the timeline, because they are made one at a time.

How do I keep it?+

Store it flat in a box rather than hanging. If it needs cleaning, hand wash cool, press the water out and dry it flat in the shade.
