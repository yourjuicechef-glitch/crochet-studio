Crochet Size Guide

Use this guide before ordering crochet clothing. Because handmade crochet can stretch differently depending on the stitch and yarn, always compare the measurements shown on the individual product page with a garment that fits you well.

How to measure

  • Chest or bust: Measure around the fullest part.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Length: Measure from the shoulder or waist as specified on the product page.

Measurements are provided in centimetres and inches where possible. If you are between sizes or need a tailored fit, contact us before ordering or request a custom crochet order.

Where to measure

Lay the garment flat and measure edge to edge, then compare it with something you already wear. These are the points every measurement on a product page refers to.

Sizes are made to your measurements

Crochet stretches differently with every stitch and yarn, so rather than publish a standard size table we work to the measurements you send. Take the three above, tell us what you would like made, and we will confirm the finished measurements before starting. Every product page lists its own measurements where the piece is a fixed size.
