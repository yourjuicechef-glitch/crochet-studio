Custom orders

Bring your idea to life.

Crochet Studio creates custom clothing, accessories, toys, gifts and home décor for customers across India. Dispatched within 5–7 days of your order. Made-to-order pieces are confirmed by WhatsApp before work starts.

What can be made to order

  • Men's and women's crochet clothing
  • Colours, sizes and design details changed to suit you
  • Crochet toys, keychains, coasters and home décor
  • Personalised gifts for birthdays, weddings and occasions

How it works

  1. 1

    Send the idea

    Your idea, a reference image, the colours you want and the size you need.

  2. 2

    We confirm what is possible

    Feasibility, price and a production timeline, in writing, before anything is agreed.

  3. 3

    Work begins

    Your order starts after you approve the design and pay the advance.

  4. 4

    Updates, then dispatch

    We send progress pictures before it is packed and sent.

Because every custom piece is handmade, production time depends on the design and quantity. Custom-made products may not be eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or differ materially from the approved design — see returns and cancellations.
