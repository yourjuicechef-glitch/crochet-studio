Frequently Asked Questions

What does Crochet Studio sell?+

We sell handcrafted crochet clothing for women and men, bags, keychains, coasters, toys, accessories and other giftable products.

Do you accept custom crochet orders?+

Yes. You can request preferred colours, sizing and design details. Feasibility, price and production time are confirmed before the order is finalised.

Can I place a bulk or corporate gifting order?+

Yes. We offer bulk crochet gifting for employees, clients, events and celebrations. Personalisation may be available depending on quantity and timeline.

How long does a handmade order take?+

Processing time varies by product, complexity and quantity. Ready-to-ship and made-to-order timelines are communicated before dispatch.

How do I choose the right clothing size?+

Please review our Crochet Size Guide. For custom garments, contact us with your measurements before ordering.

How should crochet products be washed and stored?+

Follow the instructions supplied with the product and see our Crochet Product Care Guide. Gentle washing and careful storage help maintain shape and texture.

Do you ship internationally?+

Not as a standard checkout option at present. Selected international shipping may be introduced later.

How can I contact you?+

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 98183 65369 or follow @crochetstudio.in_ on Instagram.

Not answered here? Ask us directly.

Not sure which one?
