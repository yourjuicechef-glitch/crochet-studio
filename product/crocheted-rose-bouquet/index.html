Questions about this piece

Will mine look exactly like the photograph?+

Close, not identical. Each crocheted rose bouquet is worked by hand, so the design and colour match the photograph while the size and finish vary a little from one piece to the next. Because each piece is worked by hand, sizes and shades vary a little from one to the next. That variation is the piece, not a defect.

Do the flowers need water?+

No. They are cotton yarn, so they keep their shape and colour indefinitely — dust them with a dry brush and keep them out of strong sunlight.

Can I have it in a different colour or size?+

Yes — most pieces are made to order. Send the colour, size or change you want through the custom order page and we will confirm whether it is possible, what it costs and how long it takes before anything starts.

When will it be dispatched?+

Dispatched within 5–7 days of your order. Made-to-order pieces are confirmed by WhatsApp before work starts.

How do I wash it?+

Hand wash gently in cool water with a mild detergent, never wring or bleach it, reshape it while damp and dry it flat out of direct sunlight. The care guide has the full instructions.
